- **R0.3 The fork's CI.** `ci.yml` stays on push and PR. `flink-suite.yml` and `fork-ci-status.yml` lose their `schedule:` triggers (owner rule of 2026-10-28: no scheduled CI on GitHub) and keep `workflow_dispatch`; Task 1 does this. `docs.yml`, `flink-suite-line.yml`, `jvm-aggregates.yml`, `nested-scalars.yml`, `paimon1.yml` and `release.yml` stay as they are, until a run shows they cost too much. GitHub had not registered the fork's workflows before its first push (checked 2026-10-29).
- **R0.4 Merging in the fork.** The fork's `main` has no ruleset and no branch protection (checked 2026-10-29). The coordinator merges fork PRs when CI is green; implementers never merge their own PRs.
